# Daily — "การ์ดเดิม เงินของคุณ" (motionmaxxing, 15 s, 1080x1920, 30 fps, 128 BPM)

**PAGE.** Problem: travellers and students abroad juggle currencies and apps in other languages. Audience: young people who travel / study abroad (BRIEF priority TH, JP). Goal: try Daily on the web. Emotion: "it already speaks my money".
**One true thing:** the same Daily home screen in Thai, Japanese, English and Korean shows the balance in ฿, ¥, $ and ₩ (real localized screenshots, demo data).

## Ideas
1. **Card cut across languages** (chosen): the camera holds the real balance card; hard cuts on beats swap the language and currency underneath; the lemon carries in and out. Only Daily has the lemon on its card, and the claim is performed by real screens.
2. Typographic list of currencies: rejected, invented-looking and type-only.
3. A traveller's day (coffee in Tokyo, subway in Seoul): rejected, needs screens we don't have per city.
